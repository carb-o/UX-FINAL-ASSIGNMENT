Frequently Asked Questions

Players of all skill levels are welcome! We categorize courts by skill level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced) so everyone gets fair and fun games.

Shuttlecocks are provided for official community sessions. Just bring your own racquet and non-marking court shoes!

We host weekly games across OCBC Arena (Central), Heartbeat@Bedok (East), Jurong East Sports Centre (West), and Yishun Sports Hall (North).

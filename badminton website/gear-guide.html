Racquet Weight & Balance Breakdown
Weight Classes (U Rating):

Racquets are measured using 'U'. The higher the number, the lighter the racquet:

  • 3U (85g - 89g): Standard heavy racquet for maximum smash power.
  • 4U (80g - 84g): Most popular all-around weight class for doubles speed.
  • 5U (75g - 79g): Ultra-light weight designed for minimal wrist strain.
Balance Points:

Head-Heavy: Heavy top for smashing.
Head-Light: Light top for fast defense.

String Tension Dynamics
Why String Tension Matters:

String tension changes how power is transferred from your swing to the shuttlecock:

  • Lower Tension (20-23 lbs): Gives a wider sweet spot and trampoline repulsion. Great for beginners who need power assistance.
  • Higher Tension (26-30 lbs): Gives maximum shuttle control and feel, but requires strong wrist technique and precise contact.
Court Shoe Technology
Non-Marking Rubber Soles:

Standard running shoes lack lateral support and can cause ankle rolls. Specialized court shoes feature:

  • Gum Rubber Sole: Sticky grip on synthetic and wooden court mats.
  • Lateral Claw Stabilizer: Prevents shoe rollover during extreme side-steps.
  • Power Cushioning: Absorbs heel impact when landing after overhead jump smashes.
Feather vs. Nylon Shuttlecocks
Aerodynamic Differences:
  • Feather (Goose/Duck): Used in competitive play. Decelerates sharply near the end of flight, allowing tight net spinning drops.
  • Nylon (Synthetic): Extremely durable and affordable for casual practice, but moves faster through the air without natural deceleration.
